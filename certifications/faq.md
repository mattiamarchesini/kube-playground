**Other FAQs**:

**Q**. How much to pass the real exam?

**A**. You only need 66% to clear the real exam and you also have a free retake.

**Q**. How much time does it take to get results after the exam?

**A**. Results will be emailed within 48 hours.

**Q**. Is a course and mock exams sufficient for the exam?

**A**. If you practice the practice tests and mock exams enough times and make peace with the
Kubernetes documentation pages, you should be good.

**Q**. Is auto-completion available in the exam environment?

**A**. Yes.

**Q**. How do I switch between clusters/environments in the exam environment?

**A**. The command to switch to the right cluster/context will be given at the top of each question.
Make sure you run that always. Even if you think you are on the right cluster/context!

## Online (Live) Proctored Test Requirements

It is important that you meet the minimum computer system requirements to take the online test.
Failure to meet the requirements and follow the recommended testing environment may result in
failure to complete the test and the possible forfeiture of your registration fee.

The Linux Foundation has a zero tolerance rule in regard to anyone who engages in Exam Misconduct.
The exams are administered under strict supervision and exam rules, and are carefully monitored
during the live exam, and upon completion of the exam, the session recording is reviewed. Any
suspicious activity will be addressed and will result in consequences including, but not limited to,
revoking a passing score and restricting an individual’s ability to take an exam again.

Please read the requirements and confirm that you understand the following.

**Minimum Computer System**

A personal computer with Microsoft Windows 10 or later, an Apple computer with macOS 12 or later, or
a computer with Ubuntu 20 or later. A microphone connected or built into the computer. Speakers
connected or built into the computer. A headset or headphones are NOT permitted. A camera connected
or built into the computer. An Internet connection speed of 3 Mbps or higher. Administrative access
to your device.

If you are on the device you plan to use for the test please run our quick system check. You can
also run the system check at a later time. https://syscheck.bridge.psiexams.com/en

**Your Testing Environment**

The exam must be taken in a private space, not in an open public area. You must remain in your work
area (desk or table). The work area must be well-lit, free of clutter, and free of objects unrelated
to the test.
